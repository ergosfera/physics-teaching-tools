Ergosfera Teaching Tools

A collection of free, open web apps for physics teaching and office work. No installation required — runs entirely in your browser.

⏲️Class Timer
Manage class activity segments — presentations, group work, breaks, exams — each with its own countdown.

Split-panel freehand notebook with a scientific calculator, for in-class problem solving side by side.

Minimalist full-screen timer made of a grid of dots (1s–6h). Dots fade out as time runs down — no numbers.
