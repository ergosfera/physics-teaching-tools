🔦 Reading Window

Shades the whole screen except a clear window that follows the pointer, so only what you're reading stands out. Made to help readers who are easily distracted, including people with ADHD.

💻 Desktop app Windows 10 / 11 macOS 11 or later Free · MIT License

1About

What it does

  • Everything on the screen is darkened except a clear window. You choose how dark (10% to 95%) and the shade color: black, navy, sepia, dark green… or any color.
  • The clear window can be a rectangle, a rounded rectangle, an ellipse, or a full-width reading band for going line by line. Set its width and height in the panel.
  • The clear window follows the pointer. You can still scroll the page while it follows.
  • Click to hold it where it is. The click also reaches the page, for example to place the cursor. While it's held, you can click, select and scroll inside it as usual.
  • Click the dark area to grab it again, and it follows the pointer once more. While it's held, drag just outside its edge to resize it.
  • The small panel stays on top. Shade: On/Off turns the shade off and on, and the clear window stops following while the pointer is over the panel, so its controls are easy to use.
  • It covers every screen, including the menu bar and the Dock or taskbar.

Privacy

Reading Window collects no personal data and never connects to the internet. It doesn't capture or record your screen. It only notices where the pointer is and when you click, to move and hold the clear window, and saves its own settings on your computer: the shape, size, darkness and color, and the panel position.

Installing

  • Windows: double-click ReadingWindow.exe. No installation is needed. The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: open ReadingWindow.dmg and drag Reading Window into Applications. The app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so the first time macOS may block it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
