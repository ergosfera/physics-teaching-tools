📅 Clock Tile

A small tile for the Windows taskbar that you can read from across the room: the hour and minute on a flip clock, and below it a tear-off calendar page with the month and the day.

💻 Desktop app Windows 10 / 11 Free · MIT License

1About

What it does

  • The hour and minute sit on two dark flip cards, and a calendar page below shows the month on a blue band and the day in big blue numbers.
  • It's made for a free spot on a vertical taskbar, but it can sit anywhere on the screen. Drag it into place once and it remembers where it goes.
  • Click it for a month calendar. Hover over it for the full date.
  • It stays on top of the taskbar, and hides itself while a full-screen window (a video, a slide show) is in front.
  • Right-click it for a 24-hour or 12-hour clock, six sizes from 40 × 70 to 100 × 175 pixels, Lock position, Start with Windows and Quit.
  • The month is written in the language of Windows (OCT, ENE…).

Privacy

Clock Tile collects no personal data and never connects to the internet. It only reads the computer's clock. Its position, size and options are kept in your Windows user settings.

Installing

  • Windows: double-click ClockTile.exe. No installation is needed. The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. The tile appears on the taskbar near the clock; drag it to where you want it.
