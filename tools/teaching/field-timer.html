Field is a tribute to Untime, the timer app made in 2012 by Perceptor, the Dutch design studio of Bastiaan Terhorst and Casper Schipper.

Showing time as a quiet field of dots was their idea, and the numbers drawn with dots and the glow at the end come from their app. Thank you for a wonderful idea.

Untime on perceptor.nl

Drag down on the field to set the time (or scroll, or use ↑ ↓), then press start. While it runs only the dots show: click anywhere or press Space to pause and see the time. Esc resets.
