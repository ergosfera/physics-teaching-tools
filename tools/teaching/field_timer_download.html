⚫ Field

The Field dot timer as a desktop app: a quiet field of dots that go dark as time runs out, with big numbers drawn in dots.

💻 Desktop app Windows 10 / 11 macOS 11 or later Free · MIT License
A thank-you. Field is a tribute to Untime, the iPhone and iPad timer made in 2012 by Perceptor, the Dutch design studio of Bastiaan Terhorst and Casper Schipper. Showing time as a field of dots was their idea, and the numbers drawn with dots and the glow at the end come from their app. Thank you for a wonderful idea. (Untime on perceptor.nl)

Setting the time, counting down, and time's up, shown here in the web version. The desktop app draws the same field in a window you can resize or make full screen.

1About

What it does

  • Drag down on the field (or scroll) to set the time, from 1 second to 6 hours. The gray dots fill the field from the top, and big dot numbers show the time.
  • Press start: the whole field lights up, whatever the time, and the dots go dark one by one from the end. Only the dots show while it counts down, so the class sees time passing at a glance.
  • Click anywhere to pause: the numbers and the resume and reset buttons come back. When the time is up the field glows, with an optional sound.
  • The ··· button (or a right-click) has full screen for a projector, always on top, the sound option and About.
  • Keyboard: Space start/pause, Esc cancel, ↑/↓ change the time, F11 (Windows) or ⌃⌘F (Mac) full screen.

Privacy

Field collects no personal data and never connects to the internet. It only saves its own settings (the last time you set, the sound and always-on-top options, and the window position) on your computer.

Installing

  • Windows: double-click Field.exe. No installation is needed. The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: open Field.dmg and drag Field into Applications. The app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so the first time macOS may block it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
