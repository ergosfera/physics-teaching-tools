🕑 Clock Bar

The date and time, like “April 5 2:34”, in bold outlined letters on a see-through bar that stays on top of everything. Put it at the top of the screen and it's always in view.

💻 Desktop app Windows 10 / 11 macOS 11 or later Free · MIT License
This is a download, not a web page. Unlike the other tools on this site, Clock Bar is a program that you download and run on your own computer. Before downloading, please read the About and the License below. The download button unlocks once you have scrolled through the license and accepted it.

The bar has no background of its own: the outlined letters read over light, dark and busy windows.

1About

What it does

  • Shows the date in amber (“April 5”, no year) and the time in white (“2:34”, a 12-hour clock without AM/PM), in bold letters with a dark outline.
  • The bar itself is see-through, and it stays in front of other windows, the taskbar and the Mac menu bar.
  • Drag it into place once, for example at the top of the screen; it remembers where it goes.
  • Click it for a month calendar. Hover over it for the full date.
  • Right-click it for six sizes (75 % to 250 %), Lock position, Start with Windows (Open at Login on a Mac) and Quit.

Privacy

Clock Bar collects no personal data and never connects to the internet. It only reads the computer's clock. Its position, size and options are kept in your user settings on your computer.

Installing

  • Windows: double-click ClockBar.exe. No installation is needed. The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. The bar appears at the top center of the screen; drag it to where you want it.
  • macOS: open ClockBar.dmg and drag Clock Bar into Applications. The app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so the first time macOS may block it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The bar appears just under the menu bar, in the middle; it has no Dock icon, so right-click it to quit.
