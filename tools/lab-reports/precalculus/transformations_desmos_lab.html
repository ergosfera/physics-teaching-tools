📋 Guide: Transformations with Desmos

Read this before you start. You can open it again at any moment with the 📋 Guide button at the top.

How it works

  1. Open Desmos in another tab or window, next to this page. The green boxes tell you what to type there.
  2. There is one function, f(x) = 0.1x⁴ − 0.75x³ + x² + 2x − 1, and one slider, a. You change f in four ways and find out what each one does to the graph.
  3. Predictions (purple) come before you move the slider. Save your honest guess: it is never marked wrong.
  4. For the other questions, answer first, then press Check. If you are not right, read the hint, look at Desmos again and try again. After two wrong tries you can see the answer, but try first: your teacher sees how many tries you needed.

Typing answers

Type numbers like 2.6, −1 or 1/2. A point has two boxes: x in the first one and y in the second one.
