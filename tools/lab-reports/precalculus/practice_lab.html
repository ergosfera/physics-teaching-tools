📋 Guide

Read this before you start. You can open it again at any moment with the 📋 Guide button at the top.

How it works

  1. There are 10 questions. Answer first, then press Check. If you are not right, read the hint and try again.
  2. After two wrong tries you can see the answer, but try first: your teacher sees how many tries you needed.
  3. Some questions show a graph. Zoom out to see the end behavior, and move the mouse over it to read values.
  4. At the end, explain the main idea in your own words. Your teacher may share some answers with the class.

Typing answers

Type numbers like 3, −2 or 1/2. When a question asks for a list, separate the numbers with commas: 3, −2.
