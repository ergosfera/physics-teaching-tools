📋 Guide: Rational Functions

Read this before you start. You can open it again at any moment with the 📋 Guide button at the top.

Goal

Understand what the factors of a rational function tell you about its graph: its end behavior, zeros, vertical asymptotes and holes, how to rewrite it with long division, and how transformations move it. (AP Precalculus, topics 1.7 to 1.12.)

How it works

  1. Each section has a function and a few questions. Answer first, then press Check. If you are not right, read the hint and try again.
  2. After two wrong tries you can see the answer, but try first: your teacher sees how many tries you needed.
  3. Use the graph to check your thinking. Zoom out to see the end behavior; move the mouse over the graph to read values.
  4. Each section ends with an explanation in your own words. Your teacher will share some of them with the class.
  5. At the end, you build your own rational function with the features we give you.

Writing functions

Type ^ for powers and use parentheses: (x^2 - 1)/((x - 2)(x + 3)). You can write 3(x - 1) without a * sign.
